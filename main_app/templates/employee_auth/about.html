About the Employee Portal

A modern platform that gives employees secure, fast, and convenient access to their records, attendance, and payroll information.

Purpose of the Portal

GovHRPay Employee Portal is a self-service system that allows employees to access their employment information anytime, anywhere.

It integrates HR and Payroll systems into one platform—eliminating manual requests and improving accessibility to employee records.

This system enhances transparency, efficiency, and communication within the organization.

About Norzagaray, Bulacan

Norzagaray is a 1st class municipality in Bulacan known for its natural beauty, including Angat and Ipo Dam.

Established in 1860, it continues to evolve through digital transformation and improved public service systems.

Systems like GovHRPay reflect the municipality’s commitment to innovation, transparency, and efficiency.
