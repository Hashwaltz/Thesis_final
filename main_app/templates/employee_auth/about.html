About the Employee Portal

Providing employees with secure and convenient access to their personal records, attendance history, and payroll information.

Purpose of the Portal

```

The GovHRPay Employee Portal is a self-service platform designed to give government employees convenient access to their personal employment records. Through this portal, employees can securely view their profile information, attendance history, payroll records, and other work-related information.

By integrating the portal with the Human Resource and Payroll systems, employees can stay informed about their employment details without needing to request records manually from HR offices. This promotes transparency, efficiency, and improved communication between employees and the administration.

```

About Norzagaray, Bulacan

```

Norzagaray is a 1st class municipality located in the province of Bulacan, Philippines. It is well known for its scenic landscapes, historical heritage, and natural attractions such as Angat Dam and the Ipo Dam watershed area.

Established in 1860, Norzagaray has grown into a vibrant community committed to progress and innovation. The municipality continues to strengthen its governance through digital transformation and modern public service systems.

The implementation of systems like GovHRPay and the Employee Portal reflects the local government's commitment to improving efficiency, transparency, and accessibility of services for its workforce.
```
