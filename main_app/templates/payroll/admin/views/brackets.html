Income Tax (BIR / TRAIN Law)

Monthly taxable income is computed after contributions.
Tax brackets:

  • 0 – 20,833 → 0%
  • 20,833 – 33,332 → 20% excess over 20,833
  • 33,333 – 66,666 → 25% excess over 33,332
  • 66,667 – 166,666 → 30% excess over 66,666
  • 166,667 – 666,666 → 32% excess over 166,666
  • 666,667+ → 35% excess over 666,666
