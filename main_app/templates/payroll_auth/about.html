GovHRPay Payroll

A Web-Based Payroll Management System designed to automate salary computation, deductions, and payslip generation for government employees in Norzagaray.

System Overview

GovHRPay Payroll is a modern payroll system designed to ensure accurate salary computation and efficient payroll processing.

It integrates employee attendance, government deductions, and benefits into one streamlined platform.

The system enhances transparency, reduces manual errors, and ensures timely salary distribution.

Core Modules

Payroll Processing

Automates salary computation based on attendance and employee data.

Deductions Management

Handles taxes, GSIS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and other deductions automatically.

Payslip Generation

Generates detailed and downloadable employee payslips.

Attendance Integration

Syncs attendance records directly into payroll computation.

Payroll Reports

Provides summaries, analytics, and payroll history reports.

Data Security

Ensures secure handling of financial records and employee payroll data.

System Impact

GovHRPay Payroll improves efficiency in payroll operations by automating complex computations and minimizing manual errors. It strengthens financial transparency and ensures that employees receive accurate and timely compensation.

Developed By

GovHRPay Payroll is part of a digital governance initiative aimed at modernizing payroll systems in Norzagaray, Bulacan through a secure and efficient platform.
