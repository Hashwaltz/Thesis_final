GovHRPay System

A Web-Based Human Resource and Payroll Management System designed to automate employee records, payroll processing, and HR operations efficiently.

System Overview

GovHRPay is a centralized Human Resource Information System (HRIS) combined with payroll management features. It is designed to digitalize HR workflows and reduce manual processing.

The system handles employee management, attendance tracking, payroll computation, deductions, and reporting in a single platform.

It improves accuracy, efficiency, and transparency in HR and payroll operations.

Core Modules

Employee Management

Manage employee profiles, employment details, and department assignments.

Payroll System

Automated salary computation with deductions and benefits handling.

Attendance Monitoring

Tracks daily attendance and integrates data into payroll processing.

Reports Generation

Generates payroll summaries, employee reports, and analytics.

User Authentication

Secure login system with role-based access control.

System Security

Ensures data protection and controlled access to sensitive HR records.

System Impact

GovHRPay reduces manual workload in HR departments by automating payroll and record management. It minimizes errors, speeds up processing, and improves transparency in employee compensation and data handling.

Developed By

GovHRPay is a capstone project developed to modernize Human Resource and Payroll systems through a secure and efficient web-based platform.
